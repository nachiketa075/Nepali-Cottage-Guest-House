# Research notes: Nepali Cottage Guest House, Lakeside, Pokhara

These notes cover the online research behind the website content. They were collected on **17 October 2026** (UTC).
Listing pages change often, so recheck anything time-sensitive (ratings, prices, policies) before relying on it.

**How items were published.** A fact went onto the website only if one of these was true:

- At least two independent sources agree on it.
- It comes from owner-supplied material (the photos, videos and prices you provided).
- It is the property's own listing text, written in the owner's voice, and nothing contradicts it.

Single-source and conflicting items are listed under **Unresolved** for the owner to confirm. Booking.com and
bedandbreakfast.eu count as one source, because bedandbreakfast.eu is a Booking.com partner that mirrors its data.
Expedia, Hotels.com and Travelocity also count as one source between them.

## Room categories and prices

The owner supplied two prices: **Standard Room 1,600** and **Deluxe Room 1,700**. These override every third-party
price.

- **Currency, basis and inclusions: not confirmed.** Taxes, breakfast and occupancy are also unconfirmed.
  - Google showed third-party prices of ₹953–₹969 (INR) for 25–26 October 2026. That is roughly consistent with NPR
    1,600–1,700 per night.
  - This is only an observation. The site does not assume a currency or a per-night basis.
- **Booking.com room types:**
  - "Standard Twin Room": 194 ft² / 18 m², 2 single beds.
  - "Deluxe Double or Twin Room": 226 ft² / 21 m², garden view per the partner mirror.
- **Agoda room types:** eight names, including "Basic Room", "Standard Room" and "Deluxe Room" (10 m², max 4 adults).
  Several share one generic photo.
- **Room counts:** Agoda and the Booking.com partner mirror both say 8 rooms. Inventory per category is unknown, so no
  count is published.

## Unresolved: owner to confirm

1. **Check-in and check-out times conflict.**
   - Booking.com: from 12:00, out by 11:30.
   - Agoda: from 12:00, check-in until 22:00, out by 12:00. Agoda also lists a "24-hour" check-in and front desk, and
     "Reception open until 10:15 AM".
   - Hostelworld: 11:00–22:00, out by 12:00.
   - Not published.
2. **Parking conflicts.** Booking.com says "No parking available". Agoda says free parking. Hostelworld lists free
   parking. Not published.
3. **Airport transfer conflicts.** Agoda says it is available for an extra charge and gives a fee of "5 NPR", which
   looks wrong. Hostelworld lists "Airport Transfers". Booking.com doesn't say. Not published.
4. **Airport distance is not published.**
   - Listings give "Pokhara Airport (PKR) 2.4 km" (Agoda) and 1.6 mi (Booking.com). That is the older domestic
     airport.
   - Since 1 January 2023, most flights use **Pokhara International Airport (PHH)**. Booking.com estimates it at
     4 mi; that is a single source.
   - The old airport still handles some Jomsom STOL and helicopter flights.
5. **Children conflict.** Booking.com says "Children are not allowed" (adults only, no cots or extra beds).
   Hotels.com gives a minimum check-in age of 18 (search snippet only). Agoda shows reviews from "families with young
   children". Not published.
6. **Pets.** Booking.com says not allowed. Agoda lists both "Pets allowed" and "Pets allowed unavailable". Not
   published.
7. **Payment conflicts.**
   - Booking.com: Cash.
   - The partner mirror (older data): cash, Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Maestro.
   - Agoda: "Cashless payment service".
   - Hostelworld shows its own card logos.
   - Not published.
8. **Breakfast.** It is available, but whether it is included in the price conflicts. Agoda says "Breakfast [free]";
   the Booking.com partner mirror says "No breakfast included" for both room types. The site says only that breakfast
   is available.
9. **Bathrooms.** Agoda lists both "Private bathroom" and "Shared bathroom", possibly for its "Basic Room". Confirm
   that both Standard and Deluxe have a private bathroom.
10. **Bed set-up and capacity per category.**
    - Booking.com shows "2 single beds" for both categories.
    - Owner photos show rooms with a double plus a single, two singles, and one double.
    - Not published.
11. **Room sizes.** 18 m² / 21 m² (Booking.com) versus "Deluxe 10 m²" (Agoda). Not published.
12. **Garden view for Deluxe.** Only Booking.com says so. Not published.
13. **Star rating.** Google says 4-star hotel. Agoda gives 1.5 stars (supplied by the property). Hotels.com gives 2.5
    stars (snippet). Booking.com's own "quality rating" is 4/5. No star rating is shown.
14. **Wendy Juice Shop.**
    - It shares the entrance sign and the phone number.
    - Booking.com lists it as the restaurant on site, and a reviewer calls it the "Wendy Juice Cafe".
    - Tripadvisor lists it as a separate restaurant (same phone, same street).
    - Ownership is not stated anywhere. The site says only that it is "at the gate" and is the café on site, and makes
      no ownership claim.
15. **WhatsApp.** No source confirms WhatsApp on +977 985-6031094. Kept hidden.
16. **Year opened.** 1995 (Agoda only); renovated 2016 (Agoda only). Not published.
17. **Laundry, bicycle hire, car hire, minimarket, hairdresser, culture classes.** All single-source (Booking.com).
    Not published.
18. **Other ratings.**
    - Agoda shows "8.5, 501 reviews" in one place and "From 150 reviews" in another.
    - Hostelworld shows "10, Superb (168)", which disagrees with its own review list.
    - Tripadvisor's 4.4 / 5 (113 reviews) was seen only via Google.
    - None of these are displayed.
19. **Guest review text.** No guest review text is reproduced. Platform terms usually forbid copying reviews; ask
    guests directly, or get permission, before quoting them.
